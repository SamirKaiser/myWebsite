About the Quote

Paul R. Halmos (1916–2006) was a renowned Hungarian-American mathematician and educator, known for his clarity of exposition and influential books such as Measure Theory and Naive Set Theory.

This quote emphasizes Halmos’s belief that mathematics is best understood through active problem-solving rather than passive reading. True comprehension comes from practice — “doing mathematics” — not just from observation or theory.

Scientific & Mathematical Projects

This section focuses on my scientific computing work: continuous-time analog solvers for MINLP and SAT; operator-theoretic and machine-learning methods for dynamical systems; and performance-oriented simulations in Rust (Barnes–Hut N-Body, 2D incompressible flow, Kuramoto–Sivashinsky). I emphasize stable integrators, convergence diagnostics, and uncertainty analysis—each repository includes reproducible notebooks, unit tests, and benchmark scripts. Explore selected results below; full methodology and source code are on my GitHub .
